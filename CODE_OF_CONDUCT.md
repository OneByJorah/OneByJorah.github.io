# Code of Conduct

This project is a calm place to build things. Everyone taking part is expected to follow a few simple rules.

## Expected behaviour

- Be respectful and assume good intent.
- Give feedback on the work, not the person.
- Be patient with newcomers and with different levels of experience.
- Accept constructive criticism gracefully.

## Unacceptable behaviour

- Harassment, insults, or discriminatory language
- Personal attacks or publishing someone's private information
- Spam, trolling, or deliberately disruptive behaviour

## Enforcement

Maintainers may edit or remove comments, close issues, or block contributors who break these rules. To report a problem, email <info@jorahone.com>. Reports are handled privately.

This policy applies in all project spaces, including issues, pull requests, and discussions.
